01 — PROFILE

About

I'm Yinqi — a multidisciplinary artist and designer working between physical and digital forms. My practice spans 3D modeling, interactive art, spatial installation, photography, electronics, and digital media.

I'm drawn to the space where technology meets the physical world, and where designed forms begin to feel personal. My work explores how objects, environments, and interactions can carry emotion and shape the way we perceive ourselves and the world around us.

I often work through experimentation rather than a fixed medium, moving between digital modeling, image-making, code, electronics, fabrication, and physical space. Across these different forms, I'm interested in creating experiences that invite curiosity, intimacy, reflection, or connection.

Much of my work begins with an exploration of the body and its relationship to objects and space — how a form can extend the body, respond to it, surround it, or transform the way it is perceived.

The pieces floating above are part of an ongoing 3D modeling study exploring wearable, body-adjacent sculpture — treating the body as structure and structure as ornament, while exploring the boundary between anatomy, object, and adornment.
