01 — PROFILE

About

I'm Yinqi — a designer and artist based between research and making. My work moves between physical and digital form: sculptural 3D objects, photography and film, spatial installation, and generative digital art. Across all of it, I'm interested in the same question — how form and material can carry feeling, and how interaction can create empathy between a person and an object, a space, or another person.

The pieces floating above are part of an ongoing 3D modeling study in wearable, body-adjacent sculpture — reading the body as structure, and structure as ornament.
