Structure,
Worn.

Eight sculptural forms — a necklace, front piece, waist piece, leg piece, bracelet, and three bangles — begin as digital sculpts. Each is then taken into the physical: 3D printed and hand-painted with a metallic finish before being fitted to the body.

Below: the finished pieces worn as a single look, the fabrication process behind them, and every individual piece in the round as a 3D model.
