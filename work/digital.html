Noise Diary: a dark room you enter through a door, a floating journal you have to find, and a password that ties the piece back to the real world.

PROJECT.01

Noise Diary  /  噪声日记

Medium: Interactive web experience — Three.js, WebGL, spatial audio Themes: Mental illness, memory, hidden emotions Date: Jun 2026

The viewer starts outside a room, clicks the door, and enters a darker space. Inside, a small journal floats somewhere in the dark. Clicking it pulls the camera in and brings the viewer into the journal itself — the private space the whole piece is built around.

The room is meant to feel uncomfortable, lonely, and confusing — like being inside a mind that is not calm. Flickering light, dark color, red lines, glass and paper textures, and layered sound all work to hold that tension. The journal stands for thoughts and memories that are hard to explain to anyone else, the kind of feelings people living with depression or schizophrenia often carry in silence.

The journal is locked. The password is 332000 — the number, in thousands, of people affected by depression worldwide. Enter it wrong and the piece answers with the question directly: how many people are affected by depression worldwide? The goal isn't to scare anyone, but to leave them curious, a little uncomfortable, and closer to the people who struggle without saying so.

  1. Click the door to enter the room
  2. Look around the dark space and find the floating journal
  3. Click the journal to move inside it
  4. Enter the password — 332000 — to open it
